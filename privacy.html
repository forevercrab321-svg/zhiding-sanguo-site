Three Kingdoms Dice: Privacy Policy

掷鼎三国 · iPhone, iPad and Android

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Three Kingdoms Dice (“the game”) is made and published by Zhule Li, an individual developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the game does with data. The short version: the game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. If you play alone or with people nearby, nothing reaches us. If you use an internet room, the match passes through our relay server, and we keep only anonymous room and match records that cannot be linked to you.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Zhule Li. Contact: support@xcity.ai. We can give a postal address on request.

What the game does not collect

  • No account, sign-up or login. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics or tracking SDK, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

Solo play and nearby play

Solo play (against the computer) and pass-and-play run entirely on your device and never connect to us.

Nearby play (“Play Together”) connects devices on the same Wi-Fi, a phone hotspot or, between iPhones and iPads, Bluetooth directly to each other. Only the player name you type, the ruler you pick and the moves of the match travel between those devices. They do not go through our servers and we never receive them. While a room is open, the host’s name is shown as the room name to other players nearby who are looking for rooms. For this, iPhone and iPad ask for Local Network and Bluetooth access; you can decline if you only play solo. The Android version uses Wi-Fi and asks for no extra permission.

Internet rooms

The game connects to our relay server only when a host taps “Open to the internet” or someone joins with a 6-letter room code. The relay is hosted by Fly.io in the United States.

  • What passes through: the player names, rulers and moves in that room, and a random rejoin number the game made on your device (it puts you back in your seat after a dropped connection). The relay forwards these between the devices in the room in real time and does not store them.
  • Your IP address: like any internet server, the relay sees your device’s IP address while you are connected. We do not store it. To stop people guessing room codes, the relay counts wrong codes per address in memory for up to one minute. Our hosting provider may process connection data briefly to deliver the connection.
  • What we keep: anonymous room and match records: when a room opened and closed, the most players it had, the game version, when a match started and ended, how many players were human, the number of seats, the winning ruler and the number of rounds. These records contain no names, device identifiers or IP addresses. They are stored in a Supabase database in the United States so we can see how the game runs and fix problems.
  • How long: the records are deleted automatically after 24 months.
  • Security: connections to the relay are encrypted (TLS), and the database is not readable from the game or the public internet.

For users in the EU and UK: to the extent this data is personal data, we process it on the basis of our legitimate interest in running the multiplayer service and keeping it working.

Game Center (iPhone and iPad)

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game uses it for achievements and leaderboards. Your Game Center nickname and scores are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive your Apple ID or any Game Center data beyond what Apple shows on the leaderboards. You can play without Game Center.

Data stored on your device

Saved games, settings (sound, speed, language) and tutorial progress are stored only in the game’s own storage on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes them. If your iPhone or iPad backs up to iCloud, these files may be included in that backup under Apple’s privacy policy. The Android version opts out of system cloud backup.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game asks for no personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. California residents have the right to know and to delete, and we will not discriminate against you for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use sensitive personal information.

How to request deletion or access: email support@xcity.ai. To be honest about what we can do: the room and match records we keep have no name, device identifier or IP address, so we cannot tell which records came from you, and we cannot find or delete them for one person. They are deleted for everyone after 24 months. Your saves and settings are only on your device, and you delete them by deleting the game. If you have emailed us, we can delete that email correspondence on request. We reply within one month.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the game does with data, we will update this page and its effective date, and update the privacy details on the App Store and Google Play before the version with the change is released.

Contact

Zhule Li · support@xcity.ai

《掷鼎三国》隐私政策

Three Kingdoms Dice · iPhone、iPad 和 Android

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

《掷鼎三国》（Three Kingdoms Dice，以下称“本游戏”）由个人开发者 Zhule Li（以下称“我们”）开发和发行。本政策说明本游戏如何处理数据。简单说：没有账号、没有广告、没有追踪。单机或和身边的人联机时，我们收不到任何东西；使用互联网房间时，对局经过我们的中转服务器，我们只保存无法对应到你的匿名房间和对局记录。

谁负责

数据控制者是 Zhule Li。联系邮箱：support@xcity.ai。如需通信地址，可来信索取。

本游戏不收集的内容

  • 不需要注册或登录，不会问你的姓名、邮箱或电话。
  • 没有广告、广告标识符、统计分析或追踪工具，不跨应用或网站追踪你。
  • 不读取通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风或位置。
  • 我们不出售、也不共享个人信息。

单机和附近联机

单机（对电脑）和同屏轮流玩完全在你的设备上进行，不连接我们。

“联机对战”让同一个 Wi-Fi、手机热点，或 iPhone / iPad 之间通过蓝牙直接互连。只在这几台设备之间传送你填的玩家名字、选的主公和每一步对局操作，不经过我们的服务器，我们收不到。房间开着时，房主的名字会作为房间名显示给附近正在找房间的玩家。为此 iPhone / iPad 会询问“本地网络”和“蓝牙”权限；只玩单机可以拒绝。Android 版使用 Wi-Fi，不需要另外授权。

互联网房间

只有房主点了“开放互联网房间”，或有人输入 6 位房间码加入时，本游戏才连接我们的中转服务器（由 Fly.io 托管，位于美国）。

  • 经过的内容：房间里的玩家名字、主公、对局操作，以及游戏在你设备上随机生成的重连编号（掉线后用它回到自己的座位）。服务器只实时转发，不保存。
  • 你的 IP 地址：和所有互联网服务器一样，连接期间服务器会看到你设备的 IP 地址。我们不保存它。为防止乱猜房间码，服务器会在内存里按地址记录输错的次数，最多一分钟。托管服务商可能为传输连接而短暂处理连接数据。
  • 我们保存的内容：匿名的房间和对局记录：开房和关房时间、最多几个人、游戏版本、对局开始和结束时间、几位真人玩家、座位数、获胜的主公、打了几天。这些记录不含名字、设备标识或 IP 地址，存放在 Supabase（美国）数据库里，用来了解游戏运行情况、修复问题。
  • 保存多久：24 个月后自动删除。
  • 安全：与服务器的连接经过加密（TLS），数据库无法从游戏或公网读取。

对欧盟和英国用户：如这些数据属于个人数据，我们处理它的法律依据是运行和维护联机服务的正当利益。

Game Center（iPhone 和 iPad）

如果你登录了 Game Center，本游戏用它提供成就和排行榜。你的 Game Center 昵称和分数由 Apple 按照 Apple 隐私政策处理。我们收不到你的 Apple ID，也收不到排行榜公开显示以外的 Game Center 数据。不登录 Game Center 也能玩。

保存在你设备上的数据

游戏存档、设置（声音、速度、语言）和新手提示进度只保存在你设备上本游戏的存储空间里，不会发给我们。删除游戏即一并删除。如果你的 iPhone / iPad 开启了 iCloud 备份，这些文件可能随设备备份由 Apple 保存，适用 Apple 的隐私政策。Android 版不参与系统云端备份。

儿童

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。本游戏不向任何人索取个人信息。如果你认为有儿童向我们发送了个人信息（例如通过邮件），请联系我们，我们会删除。

你的权利

根据你所在地区的法律（例如欧盟 GDPR、英国 GDPR、美国加州 CCPA），你可能有权访问、更正或删除与你有关的个人数据，反对或限制处理，要求数据可携，并向当地数据保护机构投诉。加州居民享有知情权和删除权，我们不会因你行使权利而区别对待。我们不出售、不共享个人信息，也不使用敏感个人信息。

如何申请删除或访问：发邮件到 support@xcity.ai。坦白说明：我们保存的房间和对局记录不含名字、设备标识或 IP 地址，所以我们无法分辨哪些记录来自你，也无法为某个人单独查找或删除；所有记录满 24 个月后统一删除。存档和设置只在你的设备上，删除游戏即可删除。如果你给我们发过邮件，可以要求我们删除这些往来邮件。我们会在一个月内回复。

政策变更

如果本游戏处理数据的方式发生变化，我们会在发布相应版本之前更新本页面和生效日期，并更新 App Store 和 Google Play 上的隐私说明。
