Frequently asked questions

How do I start a game? On the title screen tap New game, choose whether each seat is a player or the computer, pick your ruler and play. The rules are under How to play on the title screen, or pause during a match.

How do I play with friends nearby? Everyone opens Play Together. Join the same Wi-Fi or phone hotspot (iPhones and iPads can also use Bluetooth alone). One player taps Create Room; the others pick the room under Rooms Nearby, or type the 7-character room code shown on the host’s screen. Up to 4 players. On iPhone and iPad, allow Local Network (and Bluetooth) when asked.

How do I play with friends far away? The host creates a room and taps Open to the internet. The game shows a 6-letter internet room code; send it to your friends, and they type it under Join by Code. Both sides need an internet connection. If the game says it can’t reach the game server, check your connection and try again.

Where are my saves? On your device only. The game saves automatically at the start of each round (Continue on the title screen picks it up), and the pause menu has 3 manual save slots. There is no cloud save. On iPhone and iPad, saves come back if you restore the device from an iCloud or computer backup. On Android, saves are not included in cloud backups, so they stay on that phone and are removed if you uninstall the game.

How do I switch between English and Chinese? Tap 中文 / English in the top-right corner of the title screen. The game remembers your choice.

How do I report a player? The host can remove a player from a room with Remove. To report an offensive player name or behaviour, email forevercrab321@gmail.com with the room code, the player name, and the date and time. We read every report. We do not store player names, so we cannot look up a player afterwards, but reports help us improve name filtering and safety features.

What data does the game collect, and how do I delete it? The game has no accounts, ads or tracking, and collects no personal data. Internet rooms keep anonymous room and match records (no names, device IDs or IP addresses), which are deleted after 24 months; because they can’t be linked to you, we can’t pick out yours. Your saves and settings are only on your device: delete the game to delete them. Details are in the privacy policy.

The sound is too loud or too quiet. Settings (title screen or pause menu) has separate sliders for music, sound effects, interface and voice. On iPhone, the silent switch also mutes the game.

Does the game cost anything? Three Kingdoms Dice is free to download, with no ads and no in-app purchases in this version.

常见问题

怎么开始？ 主界面点“新游戏”，选择每个座位是玩家还是电脑，再选主公即可开战。规则在主界面“玩法”里，对局中也可以暂停查看。

怎么和身边的朋友联机？ 大家都打开“联机对战”，连同一个 Wi-Fi 或手机热点（iPhone / iPad 之间也可以只开蓝牙）。一人点“创建房间”，其他人在“附近的房间”里选，或输入房主屏幕上的 7 位房间码。最多 4 人。iPhone / iPad 询问“本地网络”（和“蓝牙”）权限时请允许。

怎么和不在身边的朋友联机？ 房主创建房间后点“开放互联网房间”，游戏会显示 6 位互联网房间码；发给朋友，朋友在“用房间码加入”里输入。双方都要能上网。如果提示连不上联机服务器，请检查网络后再试。

存档在哪？ 只在你的设备上。每回合开始自动存档（主界面“继续上一局”接着玩），暂停菜单还能手动存到 3 个存档位。游戏不做云存档。iPhone / iPad 从 iCloud 或电脑备份恢复设备时存档会一起恢复；Android 版存档不进云端备份，只留在这台手机上，卸载游戏会一并删除。

怎么切换中文和英文？ 点主界面右上角的“中文”/“English”。游戏会记住你的选择。

怎么举报玩家？ 房主可以在房间里点“请离”让某位玩家离开。如要举报不当的玩家名字或行为，请发邮件到 forevercrab321@gmail.com，写上房间码、玩家名字和日期时间。每封举报我们都会看。我们不保存玩家名字，事后无法查到某位玩家，但举报能帮助我们改进名字过滤和安全功能。

游戏收集什么数据？怎么删除？ 没有账号、广告和追踪，不收集个人信息。互联网房间会保存匿名的房间和对局记录（不含名字、设备标识或 IP 地址），24 个月后删除；因为无法对应到你，我们也无法单独挑出你的记录。存档和设置只在你的设备上，删除游戏即可删除。详见隐私政策。

声音太大/太小？ 主界面或暂停菜单的“设置”里可以分别调音乐、音效、界面和语音音量。iPhone 静音键打开时游戏也会静音。

要花钱吗？ 《掷鼎三国》免费下载，没有广告，本版本没有内购。
